About us

Small batches, short lists, plants you can name.

We started with a simple belief: everyday care works best when it is honest and made from things you recognise. Every herb, oil and tea we sell follows traditional methods, prepared slowly and packed with care.

Short, readable ingredient lists. Every herb is named and nothing is hidden behind “fragrance”.

Made with care

Small batches and traditional recipes, so each jar is fresh and consistent.
